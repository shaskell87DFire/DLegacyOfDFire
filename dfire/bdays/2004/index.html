Happy Birthday
by The Faerie Keeper/Spirit Keeper

Your day of birth has arrive this year,
so have we who hold you so dear;
Laiden with presents and sweet cake,
piled high, all of which you may take.

Take to bless you in a special way,
and cards written which always say,
thank God for your special birthday.

Elo Fuddpucker brought a bike,
red, white and blue, which you like;
Byrtha has fixed your Barbie doll,
and Smeelie hopes you do not fall.

During the party you ate too much cake
your hands were sticky, for goodness sake
and Elmo told you not to ride the bike,
but remember you told him to take a hike.

Well, things ended up really all right,
sorry you had to stay up late at night;
We all signed the guestbook which said,
hope you soon get up from your bed.

Things will be much better next year,
from your friends, you have nothing to fear;
We all love you and wish you good luck,
and sorry we left you in such a big muck.

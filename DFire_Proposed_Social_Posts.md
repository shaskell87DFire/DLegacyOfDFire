Recurring Preservation Themes to Reinforce

These can be woven naturally into future posts without repeating them every time.

Original files are the source of truth.

Wayback Machine captures are used when local backups do not exist.

Missing original assets are not recreated and presented as authentic.

When modern compatibility changes are necessary, they are documented.

When existing Donna-created assets are reused on incomplete pages, that choice is disclosed.

Modern organizational choices — such as assigning a recovered page a new archival home — are identified as modern choices.

AI may assist with technical restoration, troubleshooting, organization, and documentation, but it does not determine the historical record.

Personal pages belonging to other Site Fighters are returned privately when appropriate and are not automatically turned into content.

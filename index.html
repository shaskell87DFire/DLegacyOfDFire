D'Fire's World Wide Web of Yesteryear 🔥🦅

Preserving D'Fire's little slice of the Internet of the early 2000s.

Welcome! This site is dedicated to preserving the online world created and loved by D'Fire — my mom — during the early days of the World Wide Web.

Long before social media became the Internet we know today, people built their own little corners of the web. They made graphics, wrote HTML, joined online communities, signed guestbooks, traded gifts and awards, and formed friendships with people from all over the world.

D'Fire was part of that world. She created websites and graphics, wrote, coded, and became deeply involved with The Site Fights and the Soaring Eagles, where many of those online friendships became very real ones.

More than two decades later, pieces of that world still survive — tucked away on old webpages, archived by the Wayback Machine, saved on old computer disks, and remembered by the people who were there.

This website is my attempt to bring those pieces back together and preserve D'Fire's little corner of the Internet so it doesn't disappear.

The site is currently under construction as I rediscover and restore her pages, graphics, writings, and memories. ❤️
